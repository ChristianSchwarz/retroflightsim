Systems

            
    
                
  • G: Landing gear
    • 
                
  • F: Flaps

Views

Time of day

            

                Local solar time. Moves the sun, so sky, terrain and cast shadows follow it.
                Sunrise 06:00, sunset 18:00. N flips between afternoon and midnight.

Area

            

                Where in the world to fly. Areas other than the home one are
                terrain only — no airbase, no carrier — so you start
                airborne over the middle of them. Changing this reloads the sim.
            

            

                
                
            


            
Terrain colour

            

                Every facet is baked with both a landcover class and a satellite
                colour; this picks which one paints it.
